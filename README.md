## Critical data definitions

The Olist order-items source contains `order_item_id` but **no quantity field**.

Therefore:

**Merchandise revenue = sum(price) across eligible order lines**

The project does not create a quantity-based calculation that the source cannot support.

Distinct orders are controlled separately from line-level revenue so multi-category orders are not double-counted in the dashboard.

Payments and reviews are also kept at their own fact grain.

## Core analytical insights

1. **Revenue concentration:** the top five product categories contribute about 39.8% of merchandise revenue.
2. **Geographic concentration:** the top three customer states contribute about 63.4% of merchandise revenue.
3. **Retention:** observed repeat-customer rate is 3.04%.
4. **Delivery:** 93.23% of qualifying delivered orders are on time.
5. **Customer experience:** late orders have lower observed review scores than on-time orders; this is treated as an association.
6. **Freight intensity:** freight value is about 16.6% of merchandise revenue; it is used as a logistics measure.
